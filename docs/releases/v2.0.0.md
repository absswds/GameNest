### 新增游戏

- **狼人杀**：预女猎白标准板，6-12 人按人数自动配板（6 人：2 狼 + 预 + 女 + 2 民；12 人：4 狼 + 预 + 女 + 猎 + 白 + 4 民），手机当法官，带 AI。
- **三国身份局**：主忠反内身份局，4-8 人；有杀、闪、桃、酒，12 种锦囊和装备，12 名武将各有技能，带 AI。

### 狼人杀玩法

- **手机当法官**：夜间行动、警长竞选、投票、猎人开枪、移交警徽都在手机上完成，各阶段有倒计时（狼人 40 秒、女巫 25 秒、投票 25 秒、猎人 20 秒）。
- **发言方式**：房间内可选「面对面」（线下开口说）或「打字」（线上聊天）。
- **打字发言规则**：
  - 白天只有当前发言者能打字。
  - 讨论和投票阶段，所有存活玩家都能打字。
  - 夜里只有狼人能在狼队频道打字，非狼人玩家收不到这些消息。
  - 阶段切换后，旧阶段的消息会被拒收，夜里的话不会漏到白天。
- **自由讨论**：发言结束后可加一段自由讨论，时长可调，默认 60 秒，设为 0 即关闭。所有人点「准备」后提前结束。
- **规则校验**：女巫每晚最多用一瓶药，不能自救；聊天每条最多 120 字，发送间隔至少 0.8 秒。

### 三国身份局玩法

- **锦囊**：无懈可击、过河拆桥、顺手牵羊、无中生有、决斗、借刀杀人、南蛮入侵、万箭齐发、桃园结义、五谷丰登。
- **延时锦囊**：乐不思蜀、闪电。
- **装备**：9 种武器，八卦阵、仁王盾，+1 / -1 马。
- **12 名武将**：刘备、关羽、张飞、曹操、司马懿、夏侯惇、孙权、甘宁、吕蒙、吕布、华佗、貂蝉。被动技、转化技、主动技都已实现，主公技暂未实现。
- **连锁结算**：响应和无懈链按栈逐层结算；出牌阶段 40 秒，响应 15 秒，无懈 10 秒，弃牌 25 秒。
- **身份保密**：只公开主公、阵亡玩家和自己的身份，其他人的手牌只显示张数。

### MCP：让 AI 上桌

- **AI 当玩家**：新增 `mcp/` 包，Claude Desktop、Claude Code 等 MCP 客户端可以像普通玩家一样入座，走和网页相同的 WebSocket 协议。
- **两种连接**：stdio，以及 Streamable HTTP（`--http=<port>`，带 Origin 校验）。
- **完整流程**：建房、加入、加减 AI、改规则、准备、开局、看状态、等回合、走子、发消息、下一局、再来一局、退出。
- **规则与提示**：`get_rules` 给出每款游戏的走子格式，`suggest_move` 让内置 AI 给出建议着法。
- **你画我猜**：AI 能看画布（`get_canvas`）、用 SVG 作画（`draw`），也能撤销（`undo_stroke`）和清空（`clear_canvas`）。

### 优化

- **棋类多局制**：象棋、国际象棋、跳棋、黑白棋、五子棋、九路围棋可选 1 / 3 / 5 局制。每局结束后双方交换先后手，AI 跟着换座。
- **斗地主**：
  - 固定 3 人。
  - 新增「赢家先叫」选项。
  - 超时自动出牌：必须首出时出最小单张，否则不出。
- **德州扑克**：新增大盲选项，每局只打一手牌。AI 改用合法加注额，面对全下不再一律弃牌。
- **你画我猜**：画手可撤销（最多 60 笔）或清空画布，各端同步；有人猜中后 5 秒开始下一轮。
- **海战棋**：双方同时布阵；被击沉的船公开并显示轮廓。
- **扫雷**：第一下必不是雷。
- **象棋 / 围棋**：象棋三次重复局面或连续 120 步无吃子判和，AI 主动避免重复；九路围棋 200 手后自动数子，AI 不落后时会跟着虚手。
- **宽屏侧栏**：合成大西瓜、2048、扫雷、数独、羊了个羊、数字炸弹、24 点、你画我猜在宽屏下把信息放到两侧；方形棋盘类游戏改用侧边舞台布局。
- **界面重做**：魔力桥界面重做；海战棋、五子棋、24 点、麻将、合成大西瓜的布局也做了优化。
- **统一卡牌与动效**：扑克牌面、手牌滚动条、动效和设计变量统一成共享组件，动效遵循系统「减少动态效果」设置。
- **移动端**：
  - 旋转或改变窗口大小后画布自动重绘。
  - 手牌不再因每次广播而跳动。
  - 横屏小高度时自动适配。
- **人数提示**：人不够时提示「至少需要 N 名玩家（人不够可以添加 AI）」。
- **规则说明**：三十余款游戏的规则教程对照代码校正并扩写，中英同步。
- **手机移动网络**：App 能区分 WiFi / 热点 / 有线和移动数据，网络切换后几秒内自动刷新状态栏和大厅；用移动数据时也能在本机建房，大厅只显示能单人对 AI 的游戏，加上可单人玩的合成大西瓜和扫雷竞速（标注「可单人玩」）；狼人杀的 AI 无法真正交流，此时隐藏。分享的局域网地址会排除蜂窝网卡、vgate0 等点对点隧道网卡和运营商 NAT（100.64.0.0/10）地址。
- **文档与仓库**：README、架构文档更新，新增 MCP 文档（含已知限制，中英两份）；贡献指南和安全说明移到 `.github/`，与架构文档、PR 模板一起改为中英对照；GitHub 加入双语 Issue 表单、PR 模板和 Dependabot；新增 15 个测试文件。

### 修复

- **德州扑克**：盲注不再重复计入；筹码正确进入底池；加注后重新开放行动；修正全下加注校验。
- **麻将**：
  - 结算改为零和：点炮由放炮者一人赔付，自摸由仍在局中的玩家赔付。
  - 血战模式下已胡的玩家不再付钱。
  - 对对和改为按暗手判定（含红中百搭）。
- **斗地主**：修复 AI 出牌后回合计时没有重置的问题。
- **规则对齐**：
  - UNO：打出最后一张牌立即结束，包括 +4。
  - 大老二：只有开局首手必须带 ♦3。
  - 红心大战：第一墩任何人都不能出分牌；不换牌的轮次直接开打。
  - 五子棋：连成 5 子及以上即胜，满盘判和。

- **中英文对照**：补齐 5 条服务器提示的英文 / 中文（大老二首出、斗地主无效操作、无效出招、黑白棋有棋可下不能跳过、UNO 没有可质疑的 +4）；麻将渲染器出错提示改为双语。

### New games

- **Werewolf**: the standard Seer / Witch / Hunter / Idiot setup for 6-12 players, auto-balanced by count (6 players: 2 wolves + Seer + Witch + 2 villagers; 12 players: 4 wolves + Seer + Witch + Hunter + Idiot + 4 villagers). Your phone is the moderator; AI fills empty seats.
- **Three Kingdoms Roles**: a Lord / Loyalist / Rebel / Renegade role game for 4-8 players, with Strike, Dodge, Peach and Wine, 12 trick and equipment types, and 12 generals with their own skills; AI fills empty seats.

### Werewolf rules

- **Phone as moderator**: night actions, the sheriff election, voting, the hunter's shot and the badge hand-off all happen on the phone, each on a timer (wolves 40s, witch 25s, vote 25s, hunter 20s).
- **Talk mode**: choose "face to face" (speak out loud) or "chat" (typed) in the room.
- **Chat rules**:
  - During day speeches, only the current speaker can type.
  - In the discussion and vote phases, every living player can type.
  - At night, only wolves can type, in a private wolf channel that non-wolves never receive.
  - Messages from a phase that has already ended are rejected, so night talk never leaks into the day.
- **Free discussion**: an optional phase after the speeches, 60s by default and adjustable (0 turns it off); it ends early once everyone is ready.
- **Rule checks**: the Witch can use at most one potion per night and cannot save herself; chat messages are capped at 120 characters, with at least 0.8s between messages.

### Three Kingdoms rules

- **Tricks**: Negate, Dismantle, Steal, Something from Nothing, Duel, Borrowed Sword, Barbarian Invasion, Arrow Barrage, Peach Garden, Harvest.
- **Delayed tricks**: Indulgence and Lightning.
- **Equipment**: 9 weapons, 2 armors (Eight Trigrams, Benevolent Shield), and +1 / -1 horses.
- **12 generals**: Liu Bei, Guan Yu, Zhang Fei, Cao Cao, Sima Yi, Xiahou Dun, Sun Quan, Gan Ning, Lv Meng, Lv Bu, Hua Tuo and Diao Chan. Passive, conversion and active skills are implemented; lord skills are not yet.
- **Chain resolution**: responses and Negate chains resolve frame by frame on a stack. Timers: play 40s, respond 15s, Negate 10s, discard 25s.
- **Hidden roles**: only the Lord, dead players and your own role are shown; other hands show only their card count.

### MCP: AI at the table

- **AI as a player**: the new `mcp/` package lets MCP clients such as Claude Desktop and Claude Code take a seat like any player, over the same WebSocket protocol as the browser.
- **Two transports**: stdio, and Streamable HTTP (`--http=<port>`, with Origin validation).
- **Full flow**: create and join rooms, add or remove bots, set options, ready up, start, read state, wait for your turn, make moves, chat, next round, restart and leave.
- **Rules and hints**: `get_rules` gives each game's move format; `suggest_move` asks the built-in AI for a suggested move.
- **Draw & Guess**: the AI can view the canvas (`get_canvas`), draw with SVG paths (`draw`), and undo (`undo_stroke`) or clear (`clear_canvas`).

### Improvements

- **Board-game matches**: Chinese Chess, Chess, Checkers, Reversi, Gomoku and 9x9 Go support best of 1 / 3 / 5. Seats swap between games so the first move alternates, and bots follow their new seat.
- **Doudizhu**:
  - Exactly 3 players.
  - New "winner bids first" option.
  - Auto-play on timeout: the lowest single when you must lead, otherwise pass.
- **Texas Hold'em**: a new big-blind option and one hand per game. The bot uses legal raise sizes and no longer always folds to an all-in.
- **Draw & Guess**: the drawer can undo (up to 60 strokes) or clear the canvas, synced for everyone; the next round starts 5s after a correct guess.
- **Battleship**: both players place their ships at the same time; sunk ships are revealed with their outline.
- **Minesweeper**: the first click is never a mine.
- **Chinese Chess / Go**: Chinese Chess is a draw after threefold repetition or 120 moves without a capture, and the bot avoids repeating; 9x9 Go auto-scores after 200 moves, and the bot passes back when it isn't behind.
- **Wide-screen side panels**: Suika Battle, 2048, Minesweeper, Sudoku, Sheep Tile, Number Bomb, 24 Points and Draw & Guess move their info to the sides on wide screens; square board games use a side-stage layout.
- **Redesigned screens**: Magic Bridge has a new interface, and Battleship, Gomoku, 24 Points, Mahjong and Suika Battle have improved layouts.
- **Shared cards & motion**: card faces, the hand scroll bar, animations and design tokens are now shared components; animations respect the system's reduced-motion setting.
- **Mobile**:
  - Canvases redraw after rotation or resize.
  - Card hands no longer jump on every update.
  - Short landscape screens are handled.
- **Player-count hint**: when there aren't enough players, the message now says how many are needed and suggests adding AI.
- **Rule tutorials**: tutorials for 30+ games were checked against the code and expanded, in both Chinese and English.
- **Mobile data on phones**: the app now tells WiFi, hotspot and Ethernet apart from mobile data, and refreshes the status bar and lobby within seconds of a network change; on mobile data you can still host a room on the phone, and the lobby only shows games you can play solo against AI, plus Suika Battle and Minesweeper Race (marked "Solo OK"); Werewolf is hidden there, because its AI cannot really talk. Shared LAN addresses now skip cellular interfaces, point-to-point tunnels such as vgate0, and carrier-NAT (100.64.0.0/10) addresses.
- **Docs & repo**: README and architecture docs updated, plus new MCP docs (with known limitations, in English and Chinese); CONTRIBUTING and SECURITY moved to `.github/` and, with the architecture doc and PR template, are now bilingual; bilingual GitHub issue forms, a PR template and Dependabot; 15 new test files.

### Fixes

- **Hidden-info leaks (important)**:
  - On joining a room or reconnecting, the full room state used to be sent, so every hand was visible. Both now go through the per-player view; if building that view fails, the client gets an empty view instead of raw data.
  - Big Two, Doudizhu, Exploding Kittens, Liar's Bar, Old Maid, Rummikub and UNO now fully mask hands and decks.
  - Doudizhu kept a copy of every hand and the bottom cards that was sent to all clients; it is now masked.
  - In Exploding Kittens, the deck order and See-the-Future results are visible only to the player who looked.
- **Texas Hold'em**: blinds are no longer counted twice, chips go into the pot correctly, a raise reopens the action, and all-in raise validation is fixed.
- **Mahjong**:
  - Settlement is now zero-sum: a win off a discard is paid only by the discarder, and a self-draw by everyone still in the hand.
  - In Blood Battle, players who already won don't pay.
  - All Pungs is now judged on the concealed hand, Red Dragon wilds included.
- **Doudizhu**: the turn timer now resets after a bot moves.
- **Rules aligned**:
  - UNO: the game ends the moment the last card is played, including +4.
  - Big Two: the ♦3 is required only on the opening play.
  - Hearts: nobody may play point cards on the first trick, and the no-pass round goes straight to play.
  - Gomoku: five or more in a row wins, and a full board is a draw.
  - Checkers: a piece crowned mid-jump stops there, and the bot's men capture forward only.
  - 2048: a solo game that gets stuck without a 2048 tile counts as a loss.
- **Exploding Kittens**: Nope cards removed, and drawing is now a single tap; Skip under Attack cancels only one of the two turns.
- **Liar's Bar**: players with empty hands are skipped; when only one player still has cards, the next player must call the bluff; there is a 3-second pause after a reveal or a shot.
- **Old Maid**: draw from the next player who still has cards; the game ends when at most one player has cards; the bot follows the same rule; fixed a freeze when P1 discarded every card at the start, and 4 bots with no pairs passing cards around forever.
- **Da Vinci Code**: revealing your own last hidden tile knocks you out; when you stop guessing, the drawn tile goes into your hand face down.
- **Number Bomb**: the bomber starts the next round, or the next living player if the bomber is out.
- **Rummikub**: runs must stay within 1-13; a stalemate is won by the lowest rack; the bot's wild-card runs are fixed.
- **Checkers**: fixed a crash when the view was built before the board existed.
- **Chinese / English parity**: 5 server messages that were missing a translation now have both (Big Two opening play, Doudizhu invalid action, invalid move, Reversi cannot pass with a legal move, UNO no +4 to challenge); the Mahjong renderer error message is now bilingual.
