# v2.3.0 — 二十一点、大话骰、台球（中八和斯诺克），大厅改版，深浅色主题

### 新游戏

- **二十一点**：1–6 人一起对庄家，每人 1000 筹码起步。每局先同时下注（10/25/50/100/200 或全下），再轮流要牌、停牌或加倍；庄家 17 点停牌（软 17 也停），黑杰克赔 3:2。房主可以选牌的副数（1/2/4）、局数（5/10/15）和每步限时，打完最后一局筹码最多的人赢。可以加电脑玩家（三档难度）。
- **大话骰**：2–6 人，每人暗摇几颗骰子（3/4/5 颗可选），轮流叫“几个几”往上加，或者“开”上一家。开了以后亮骰数数，输的人扣一颗骰子，剩到最后的人赢。1 点默认当万能点，有人叫了“几个 1”这一轮就不再算万能。可以加电脑玩家（三档难度）。
- **台球**：两人对打，房间里选玩法。
  - **中式八球**：先打完自己的全色或花色球，再打进黑 8 就赢。开球时母球在开球线后随便摆；开球力度不够要重摆、换对方开，开球进黑 8 不算犯规（黑 8 放回）。开球后才定色，定色时双方都会弹出提示。没进球时碰球后必须有球碰库，否则犯规；犯规后对方全台任意位置摆自由球。
  - **斯诺克**：15 个红球加 6 个彩球。红球和彩球交替打：进一个红球得 1 分，然后选一个彩球来打，进了按分值得分，彩球再放回原位。红球打完后，彩球按黄、绿、棕、蓝、粉、黑的顺序依次打进。打错球、空杆、母球落袋都算犯规，对手至少加 4 分。打进最后的黑球时分数高的人赢。
  - 在台面上拖动只调方向；拖左边的球杆往下蓄力，松手出杆，拉回顶部就取消。瞄准线画出碰撞点和目标球的大致走向，右边的刻度条可以微调角度。
  - 可以加塞：在右边的大母球图上点击打位置，打出缩杆、跟杆和侧旋。
  - 球滚动时能看到花纹在转，击球、碰撞、撞库、落袋都有音效。电脑玩家等上一杆的球停稳一秒后，才慢慢转杆瞄准，并圈出要打的球（三档难度）。进球、犯规、开球违例都会在桌面上弹出提示。开球用力能把球堆打散，轻轻一碰会判违例。

### 新功能

- **横屏全屏模式**：台球、四川/广东麻将、斗地主、德州扑克、三国杀、贪吃蛇大作战改成全屏横屏玩。房间里点“点击全屏游玩”就横过来铺满屏幕（App 里连通知栏也藏起来），画面上只有游戏和左上角的返回键；返回回到房间页面，重新开始、菜单都在那里。每款都重新排了牌桌：
  - 麻将：传统牌面（万字、筒子、条子、字牌）、有厚度的立体牌，桌子中间的方位盘显示轮到谁和牌墙剩几张，右边有“听牌”提示和本局出牌记录，鬼牌开局展示后停在左上角。
  - 斗地主：手牌从大到小铺满底部，左右两家显示剩余张数，右边“记牌”看每种牌还剩几张；炸弹、王炸、春天有特效。
  - 德州扑克：椭圆牌桌，按钮和加注滑块在右边一列，发牌、下注、收底池都有动画。
  - 三国杀：其他人在顶部一排，自己的武将在右下角，手牌铺在底部。
  - 贪吃蛇：左边计分板、右边方向键，蛇身平滑移动。
- **大厅改版**：游戏卡片改成以封面为主，名字压在封面上，下面写人数和大约几分钟。顶部是“本周新游戏”大封面横幅（电脑和横拿手机时两款并排）；分类改成文字标签页，人数筛选改成“全部游戏”标题旁的一组分段按钮，选中块会滑过去。手机上往下滚时，顶栏收起成一个搜索按钮。
- **深浅色主题**：大厅右上角的太阳/月亮按钮切换，默认跟随系统，选过之后会记住。这一版只改了大厅页。
- **房间设置**：收成一行摘要，比如“叫抢 · 每局随机 · 20秒 · 3局 ›”。房主点开后从底部弹出设置面板，选项都换成了分段按钮，“准备/开始游戏”不用往下滚就能看到。
- **德州扑克加注、大富翁交易**：改成滑块，带 −/+ 按钮和“½ 底池 / 底池 / 全下”快捷按钮。
- **二十一点横屏**：横着拿手机时牌桌和操作区一屏放得下。
- **大厅人数筛选**：选中块滑动的速度和分类标签一致。
- **安卓 App 震动**：改用手机原生的震动接口，轮到你、扫雷插旗、赢了或输了都会震，每种震感不一样。顶部网址栏跟着深浅色一起变。

### 修复

- 游戏里的菜单第二次打开时会马上消失，之后每次都只闪一下。
- 大厅搜索框点进去时，不再只在左右两边出现两条橙色竖线。
- 大厅打开时不再整页往下跳；开了“减少动态效果”后，大厅里不再有动画。

# v2.3.0 — Blackjack, Liar's Dice, Pool (8-ball and Snooker), lobby redesign, light and dark theme

### New games

- **Blackjack**: 1–6 players against the dealer, 1000 chips each. Everyone bets at once (10/25/50/100/200 or all-in), then hits, stands or doubles in turn; the dealer stands on 17 including soft 17, and Blackjack pays 3:2. The host picks the number of decks (1/2/4), rounds (5/10/15) and the move timer; most chips after the last round wins. Bots available (three difficulty levels).
- **Liar's Dice**: 2–6 players, each shaking 3, 4 or 5 hidden dice. Raise the bid ("N dice showing X") or call the last bid. Everyone reveals, the loser drops a die, and the last player with dice wins. Ones are wild by default, until someone bids on ones that round. Bots available (three difficulty levels).
- **Pool**: two players; the host picks the variant in the room.
  - **Chinese 8-ball**: clear your solids or stripes, then sink the 8. Place the cue ball anywhere behind the head string for the break; a weak break is re-racked for the opponent, and an 8 on the break is re-spotted, not a loss. Groups are set after the break, with a card telling both players who has which. Without a pot, a ball must reach a cushion after contact; any foul gives the opponent ball in hand anywhere.
  - **Snooker**: 15 reds and 6 colours. Pot a red for 1 point, then nominate and pot a colour for its value; colours are re-spotted. Once the reds are gone, the colours go down in order from yellow to black. Wrong ball first, a miss or a scratch is a foul worth at least 4 to the opponent. Highest score after the final black wins.
  - Dragging on the felt only aims; pull the cue on the left edge down to charge and release to shoot, or push it back up to cancel. The aim line shows the contact point and the object ball's path, and a ruler on the right fine-tunes the angle.
  - Spin: tap the large cue ball on the right to choose where the cue strikes, for draw, follow and side spin.
  - Balls visibly roll, with sounds for the strike, ball contacts, cushions and pockets. Bots wait a second after the balls stop, then turn the cue onto their line with the target ball circled (three difficulty levels). Pots, fouls and illegal breaks are announced on the table. A firm break scatters the rack; a soft one is an illegal break.

### New features

- **Fullscreen landscape mode**: Pool, both Mahjong games, Dou Dizhu, Texas Hold'em, Sanguo and Snake Battle are now played in fullscreen landscape. Tap "Tap to play fullscreen" in the room and the table fills the screen (the Android app also hides the system bars), with only the game and a back button at the top left; back returns to the room page with Restart and the menu. Each table was laid out again:
  - Mahjong: traditional tile faces, tiles with depth, a centre compass showing whose turn it is and the tiles left, a waiting-tiles hint and a discard log on the right, and the wild tile pinned at the top left after it is revealed.
  - Dou Dizhu: the hand runs high to low across the bottom, both opponents show their card counts, and a card counter on the right shows what is left of each rank; bombs, rockets and spring get effects.
  - Texas Hold'em: an oval table with the action buttons and raise slider in a right-hand column, and animated dealing, betting and pots.
  - Sanguo: the other players in a row along the top, your general at the bottom right, and your hand along the bottom.
  - Snake Battle: a scoreboard on the left, arrow buttons on the right, and smooth snake movement.
- **Redesigned lobby**: cover-first game cards with the name over the cover and players and play time below. A "New this week" banner of large covers sits at the top (two side by side on desktop and phones held sideways); categories are text tabs and the player count is a segmented control with a sliding highlight next to the "All games" heading; on phones the top bar folds into a search button as you scroll down.
- **Light and dark theme**: the sun/moon button at the top right of the lobby switches theme. It follows the system until you pick one, then remembers it. Only the lobby uses it in this version.
- **Room settings**: folded into a one-line summary. The host taps it to open a bottom sheet with segmented options, and Ready / Start stays on screen without scrolling.
- **Texas Hold'em raise and Monopoly trade**: sliders with −/+ buttons and half-pot / pot / all-in shortcuts.
- **Blackjack sideways**: the table and controls fit on one screen with the phone held sideways.
- **Lobby player-count filter**: its highlight slides at the same speed as the category tabs.
- **Android vibration**: uses the phone's native haptics, with different patterns for your turn, flagging in Minesweeper, and winning or losing. The address bar follows the light or dark theme.

### Fixes

- The in-game menu vanished right after opening the second time, and only flashed after that.
- The lobby search box no longer shows two orange bars at its sides when focused.
- The lobby no longer jumps down while loading, and with reduced motion turned on nothing in the lobby animates.

**For developers**: the MCP server moves to the v2 SDK and MCP protocol 2026-07-28. Every tool now has a title and read-only / destructive hints, and `get_state` / `wait_for_turn` also return structured content. MCP covers Blackjack, Liar's Dice and Pool, including the Snooker option. The Android build uses NDK 27.3 and a `libnode.so` (Node 18.20.4) built from nodejs-mobile main, so every native library in the APK is 16 KB page aligned for Android 15+ devices. `scripts/shot-lobby.js` screenshots the lobby at four sizes in both themes and checks layout shift, horizontal scroll and reduced motion.
