# v2.2.0 — 谁是卧底、跑得快、快问快答，按人数找游戏，事件提示卡，摸牌提示

### 新游戏

- **谁是卧底**：4–10 人，每人看到自己的词，轮流描述后投票。结算有每轮表格，可以加电脑玩家（三档难度）。
- **跑得快**：2–3 人，先出完手牌的人赢。
- **快问快答**：1–8 人抢答，题库 650 道，结算有表格，可以加电脑玩家（三档难度）。

### 新功能

- **大厅按人数筛选**：分类下面多了一行“人数”，选 1 到 6 人以上，只列出这个人数能玩的游戏。选 1 人时列出所有能单人玩的游戏。
- **关键事件提示卡**：重要时刻在屏幕中间弹出一张卡，点一下可跳过。
  - 将军（国际象棋、象棋，被将的将/帅亮一圈红光）
  - 击沉战舰（海战）
  - 黑桃 Q 和全收（红心大战）
  - 开枪结果（骗子酒馆）
  - 无子可下（黑白棋）
  - 合出大西瓜（合成大西瓜）
  - 全押（德州扑克）
  - 炸弹和王炸（斗地主）
  - 天亮公布死讯（狼人杀）
  - 破冰（拉密）
  - 每轮投票结果，平票进 PK、无人出局也会提示（谁是卧底）
- **摸牌提示**：UNO、炸弹猫、抽鬼牌、达芬奇密码、拉密摸到牌时，中间先弹出摸到的牌，然后新牌在手牌里闪金边。抽鬼牌摸到马上凑成对的牌，会提示已经弃掉。
- **炸弹猫**：
  - 用拆除化解炸弹后，自己选把炸弹放回哪里：最上面、第 2 张、第 3 张、牌底或随机，别人看不到。
  - 有人甩锅时弹卡写明甩给了谁，被甩锅的人头像下显示“要连摸 N 张”。
- **羊了个羊可以一个人玩**：不用再加电脑玩家，准备后直接开始；一个人玩时槽位满了显示“挑战失败”。
- **贪吃蛇限时**：每局 3 分钟，时间到时最长的蛇获胜。

### 优化

- **第一次打开更快**：别人第一次用浏览器连进来时，大厅从近 5 秒缩短到 1 秒以内。
- **你画我猜**：画的过程实时同步给所有人；猜词时手机键盘不会再反复弹出；可以选轮数，词库扩充，繁体字也能猜对。
- **锄大地**：改成港式规则：
  - 至少 3 人；
  - A-2-3-4-5 算顺子，有同花顺；
  - 下家只剩一张牌时必须出最大的单张；
  - 按剩牌数 ×1/×2/×3 计分，累计分跨局保留。
- **大富翁**：掷骰动画放慢，骰子总点数显示在骰子下方，关键事件弹出提示卡。
- **2048**：方块平滑滑到新位置，再播合并和新块出现的动画。
- **达芬奇密码**：牌被猜中翻开时亮绿光；猜错后亮出自己的牌时亮红光。
- **等候室**：开始按钮旁不再重复提示“等待所有玩家准备”；单人游戏不再显示“等待玩家加入”。
- **电脑玩家难度**：UNO、数字炸弹、贪吃蛇、跑得快的简单/普通/困难重新调过。UNO、数字炸弹、跑得快很看运气，三档差距会比棋类小。

### 修复

- 返回房间后改了设置再开始，不再提示“游戏已开启”而开不了局（所有游戏都有这个问题）。
- 在大厅的局域网列表里点自己的房间，会回到原来的座位，不再变成一个新玩家。
- 电脑上的大厅：“返回房间”横幅不再挡住中/EN 按钮。
- 有人发出异常操作时，服务器不会再整个卡死，其他房间照常玩。
- iPhone Safari：大厅顶部不再消失。
- 安卓 App（Android 16）：在房间或对局里划返回会回到大厅（座位保留 5 分钟，可点“返回房间”回去），不再直接退回桌面；在大厅划返回才退出 App。

**感谢 @yiyang2001**：你画我猜的实时同步和手机键盘、锄大地港式规则、服务器防崩溃和消息限制、iPhone 大厅这几处修复都是 @yiyang2001 贡献的。

**开发者**：中文字体按 unicode-range 切片（`scripts/split-font.py`），WebSocket 限制了单条消息大小和发送频率；MCP 新增谁是卧底、跑得快、快问快答，`get_canvas` 能取到画猜的当前画面，`suggest_move` 在快问快答里能给出答案。

# v2.2.0 — Undercover, Run Fast, Quiz Race, player-count filter, event cards, drawn-card popups

### New games

- **Undercover**: 4–10 players. Everyone gets a word, describes it in turn, then votes. Per-round results table; bots available (three difficulty levels).
- **Run Fast (Paodekuai)**: 2–3 players; the first to empty their hand wins.
- **Quiz Race**: 1–8 players buzzing in on 650 questions, with a results table; bots available (three difficulty levels).

### New features

- **Player-count filter in the lobby**: a new "Players" row under the categories (1 to 6+) lists only the games that fit that many people. Choosing 1 lists every game you can play alone.
- **Event cards**: key moments pop up as a card in the middle of the screen (tap to skip).
  - Check in Chess and Chinese Chess (the king in check gets a red ring)
  - Sunk ships (Battleship)
  - The queen of spades and shooting the moon (Hearts)
  - Shot results (Liar's Bar)
  - A pass when no move is possible (Reversi)
  - Making a watermelon (Suika Battle)
  - All-in (Texas Hold'em)
  - Bombs and rockets (Doudizhu)
  - Deaths announced at dawn (Werewolf)
  - Breaking the ice (Rummikub)
  - Every vote result, including a tie going to PK and a round with nobody out (Undercover)
- **Drawn cards**: in UNO, Exploding Kittens, Old Maid, Da Vinci Code and Rummikub the cards you draw pop up in the middle, then glow in your hand. In Old Maid a card that pairs right away is marked as discarded.
- **Exploding Kittens**:
  - After a defuse you choose where the kitten goes back: on top, 2nd, 3rd, at the bottom or at random, unseen by others.
  - Attacks pop up a card naming the target, and the attacked player shows "draws N in a row".
- **Sheep Tile solo**: no bot needed any more; get ready and start. Filling the slot when playing alone shows "Challenge failed".
- **Snake Battle time limit**: 3 minutes per game; the longest snake wins when time is up.

### Improvements

- **Faster first load**: when someone first joins from a browser, the lobby now opens in under 1 s instead of nearly 5 s.
- **Draw & Guess**: drawing shows up live for everyone; phone keyboards no longer keep popping up while guessing; selectable round count, bigger word list, Traditional Chinese guesses accepted.
- **Big Two**: now follows Hong Kong rules:
  - at least 3 players;
  - A-2-3-4-5 counts as a straight, and straight flushes exist;
  - you must play your highest single when the next player has one card left;
  - scores are x1/x2/x3 by cards left, and totals carry over between games.
- **Monopoly**: slower dice roll with the total shown under the dice; key events pop up as cards.
- **2048**: tiles slide smoothly to their new place, then the merge and new-tile animations play.
- **Da Vinci Code**: a guessed tile flips with a green glow, and a wrong guesser's revealed tile glows red.
- **Waiting room**: no duplicate "waiting for everyone to be ready" next to the Start button; solo games no longer say "waiting for players".
- **Bot difficulty**: Easy/Normal/Hard retuned for UNO, Number Bomb, Snake Battle and Run Fast. UNO, Number Bomb and Run Fast depend a lot on luck, so the three levels are closer together than in board games.

### Fixes

- Changing settings after Return to room and starting again no longer fails with "game already started" (this affected every game).
- Tapping your own room in the lobby's LAN list puts you back in your seat instead of joining as a new player.
- Desktop lobby: the Return to room banner no longer covers the language button.
- A bad move from one player no longer freezes the whole server; other rooms keep playing.
- iPhone Safari: the top of the lobby no longer disappears.
- Android app (Android 16): swiping back in a room or game takes you to the lobby (your seat is kept for 5 minutes; tap Return to room to go back) instead of closing the app; swiping back in the lobby exits.

**Thanks to @yiyang2001** for the Draw & Guess live sync and keyboard fixes, the Hong Kong rules for Big Two, the server crash and message-limit fixes, and the iPhone lobby fix.

**For developers**: the Chinese font is split by unicode-range (`scripts/split-font.py`), and WebSocket messages are limited in size and rate. MCP now covers Undercover, Run Fast and Quiz Race; `get_canvas` returns the current Draw & Guess picture, and `suggest_move` answers in Quiz Race.
