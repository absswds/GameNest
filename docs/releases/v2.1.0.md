# v2.1.0 — 机器人三档难度、大富翁交易、局域网房间列表、断线不掉座、手机横屏全面适配

### 新功能

- **机器人难度**：全部游戏的电脑都支持 简单 / 普通 / 困难 三档，房主在房间设置里统一选。棋类按搜索深度和“走法质量温度”分档，其中跳棋、井字棋、四子棋、中国象棋、黑白棋、广东麻将等已用对打统计校准，目标是高一档对低一档赢七成左右。牌类按“记不记牌、算不算”分档。
- **大富翁交易**：自己回合可以向任一玩家提议交换地产和现金，对方 30 秒内接受或拒绝。盖了房子的同色组不能交易。电脑会按“能不能凑成同色组”来估值。
- **局域网房间列表**：大厅直接列出同一网络里等人的房间，点一下就进，不用再输房间号。房主可以把房间设为私密，不出现在列表里。
- **记住你**：名字和头像存在本机，下次进房自动带上。同房间重名会自动加数字后缀。房主上次选的房间设置（难度、局数等）按游戏记住，下次开房自动套用。
- **断线不掉座**：关掉网页或浏览器后 10 分钟内回到大厅，会出现“返回房间”。对局中断线保留座位 2 分钟，等候室 5 分钟。
- **手机体验**：对局中屏幕常亮；轮到你时有提示音和震动（可静音）；等候室可一键分享房间链接；对局中误关页面会弹提醒；房主离开时，新房主会收到提示。

### 优化

- **手机横屏**：棋类改为“玩家栏在左、棋盘居中、按钮在右”；2048、数独、扫雷、合成大西瓜、羊了个羊、数字炸弹、24 点、大富翁、飞行棋、斗地主、红心大战都能一屏放下。
- **触屏按钮**：静音、刷新、房间设置下拉框、达芬奇数字键、拉密排序、三国杀战报等小按钮放大到 36–44px。
- **五子棋**：手机上点一下先出预览，再点一下才落子，不怕点错格子。
- **开始游戏**：人数不够时按钮置灰，并提示可以添加 AI 补位。
- **锄大地**：平板和电脑上手牌区加宽，26 张牌不用横滑。
- **德州扑克**：改为连续多手牌局，筹码延续、庄家轮换、输光出局。
- **九路围棋**：两次虚着后进入点死子阶段，双方确认后再计分。
- **大厅底部**：加了 GitHub 仓库链接；安卓 App 里点外链会用系统浏览器打开。

### 修复

- 多处规则按官方玩法对齐，修复若干隐藏信息泄露、XSS 和事件监听泄漏。
- 广东麻将：玩家掉线超时后自动过或打出摸到的牌，不再卡住整桌。
- 德州扑克：两手之间有人离开时，其余玩家筹码不再整场重置。
- 九路围棋：计分阶段电脑不再无条件确认。
- 房主回大厅后再开新房，原房间会立刻把房主交给下一个人，不用等宽限期结束。
- 机器人兜底全部失败时改为隔 15 秒重试，不再每秒空转。
- 跳棋：AI 玩家（MCP `suggest_move`）连跳时不再出错卡住。

### New features

- **Bot difficulty**: every game's bots now have Easy / Normal / Hard, set by the host in room options. Board-game bots are tiered by search depth and move-quality temperature; several (checkers, tic-tac-toe, Connect 4, Chinese chess, Reversi, Cantonese Mahjong) are calibrated by self-play so each step up wins about 70%. Card-game bots differ in card memory and lookahead.
- **Monopoly trading**: on your turn, offer properties and cash to any player; they have 30 seconds to accept or decline. Color groups with houses cannot be traded. Bots value trades by whether they complete a color group.
- **LAN room list**: the lobby lists rooms waiting on the same network; tap one to join. Hosts can make a room private.
- **Remembers you**: name and avatar are saved on the device; duplicate names in a room get a number suffix. The host's last room options are remembered per game.
- **Keep your seat**: reopen the lobby within 10 minutes of closing the page and a "Back to room" banner appears. Seats are kept for 2 minutes mid-game and 5 minutes in the waiting room.
- **Phone comfort**: screen stays awake during games; a sound and vibration when it is your turn (with mute); one-tap share link; a warning before leaving mid-game; the new host is told when the host leaves.

### Improvements

- **Phones held sideways**: board games use a side layout; 2048, Sudoku, Minesweeper, Suika Battle, Sheep Tile, Number Bomb, 24, Monopoly, Flight Chess, Dou Dizhu and Hearts fit on one screen.
- **Touch targets**: small buttons enlarged to 36–44px.
- **Gomoku**: on touch screens, the first tap previews a stone and the second places it.
- **Start button**: disabled until enough players have joined, with a hint to add AI.
- **Big Two**: wider hand area on tablet and desktop.
- **Texas Hold'em**: continuous hands with chips carried over, rotating dealer and elimination.
- **Go 9x9**: two passes lead to a dead-stone marking phase confirmed by both players.
- **Lobby footer**: GitHub link; the Android app opens external links in the system browser.

### Fixes

- Rules aligned with the official games in many places; several hidden-information leaks, XSS issues and listener leaks fixed.
- Cantonese Mahjong no longer stalls when a player times out after disconnecting.
- Texas Hold'em chips are no longer reset when someone leaves between hands.
- Go 9x9 bots no longer confirm scoring unconditionally.
- When a host returns to the lobby and creates another room, the old room hands over the host role immediately.
- The bot fallback retries every 15 seconds instead of spinning every second.
- Checkers: MCP `suggest_move` no longer fails during a multi-jump.
